ULD Inventory Management System

An ultra-fast, lightweight, and secure Google Apps Script application designed to track Unit Load Devices (Containers & Palettes), calculate live stocks, and generate dynamic operational reports in real time.

📦

Container & Palette Tracking

Dynamic prefix selection (AKE, ALF, PAG, PMC) paired with 5-digit ULD numbers for instant transaction logging.

📊

Automated Live Stock

Real-time automatic calculation of ULD In and ULD Out movements with dedicated inventory balance tables.

📈

Dynamic Reports Engine

Generate Daily, Weekly, and Monthly summary reports categorized separately by Container and Palette types.

🛡️

Enterprise Audit & Security

Role-based access control, SHA-256 password hashing, mandatory OTP resets, and admin audit logging.
